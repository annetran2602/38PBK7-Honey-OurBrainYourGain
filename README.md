# 38PBK7-Honey-OurBrainYourGain
WELCOME TO OUR BRAIN, YOUR GAIN PORTAL🧠 - where helps you recognize your potential, bridge your skill gaps and take the next step for your career!!!
Student & Inexperienced Workers Job Portal - Our Brain, Your Gain is a platform designed to support students, inexperienced workers, and individuals entering the workforce by making it easier to discover entry-level job opportunities and improve their professional skills.

OUR GOAL
Help users save time during their job search while identifying opportunities for personal and professional growth. By analyzing and comparing users' resumes against job postings, our platform evaluates their existing skills, experience, and qualifications against employers' requirements.

Problems
- Co-op and Internships have to be searched manually on linkedin, indeed, etc
- Professional level jobs are in surplus compared to intern level
- Time consuming to read over a million job descriptions & adjust your CV/ resume in order to qualify for job requirements

OUR BRAIN, YOUR GAIN🧠 _ make the job finds you
- Scan all job positions for the most common skills required
- Allow students and workers to apply for highly matched skills jobs or entry level jobs
- Analyze your resume and match you to suitable jobs
- Receive feedback on in demand skills 
- Identify gaps and recommend bridges to hone your craft!!!
